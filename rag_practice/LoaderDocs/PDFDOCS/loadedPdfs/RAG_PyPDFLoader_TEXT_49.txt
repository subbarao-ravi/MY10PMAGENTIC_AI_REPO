33. Why Use pages_delimiter in Single Mode? 
Without a delimiter: 
Page 1 text Page 2 text Page 3 text 
may be harder to visually separate. 
With: 
pages_delimiter="\n--- PAGE BREAK ---\n" 
we can get: 
Page 1 text 
 
--- PAGE BREAK --- 
 
Page 2 text 
 
--- PAGE BREAK --- 
 
Page 3 text
