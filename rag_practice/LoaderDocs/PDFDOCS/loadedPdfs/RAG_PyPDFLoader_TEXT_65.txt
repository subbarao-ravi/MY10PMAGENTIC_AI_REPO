48. MCQ 1 
A PDF contains five pages. 
PyPDFLoader uses: 
mode="page" 
How many Document objects will it normally produce? 
A. 1 
B. 5 
C. 10 
D. 0 
Answer: B — 5 
Explanation 
Page mode normally creates one Document for each PDF page. 
Learning Conclusion 
One page, one Document — that is the simple rule of page 
mode.
